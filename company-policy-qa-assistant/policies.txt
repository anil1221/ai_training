Work From Home Policy:
Employees are allowed to work from home up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

Leave Policy:
Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days annually.
Unused leaves can be carried forward up to 10 days.

Security Policy:
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for all company systems.
Sharing passwords is strictly prohibited.

Travel Policy:
Business travel must be approved by department heads before booking.
Economy class should be used for domestic travel.

Data Privacy Policy:
Customer personal information must not be shared externally.
All sensitive data should be encrypted.

Code Review Policy:
Every pull request must be reviewed by at least one senior engineer before merging.

Laptop Usage Policy:
Company laptops should not be used for personal downloads or unauthorized software installations.

Attendance Policy:
Employees must mark attendance before 10 AM on working days.
