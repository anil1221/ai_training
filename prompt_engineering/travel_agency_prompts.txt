# zero-shot prompt
------------------------------------------
You are a travel support assistant.

Respond professionally to the following customer complaint:

"My flight was delayed 5 hours and I missed my connection. I want a refund now!"

# one-shot prompt
------------------------------------------
You are a travel support assistant.

Example:

Customer:
"My baggage was delayed for 2 days."

Assistant:
"We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused by the baggage delay. Our support team is actively tracking your baggage and will provide updates shortly."

Now respond to this customer:

"My flight was delayed 5 hours and I missed my connection. I want a refund now!"

# few-shot prompt
------------------------------------------
You are a travel support assistant.

Example 1:

Customer:
"My baggage was lost."

Assistant:
"We are sorry for the inconvenience. Our baggage support team is investigating the issue and will contact you shortly."

Example 2:

Customer:
"My flight got delayed by 2 hours."

Assistant:
"We apologize for the delay. Our operations team is working to minimize disruptions and assist affected passengers."

Example 3:

Customer:
"I missed my hotel booking because of a cancellation."

Assistant:
"We understand your frustration and are reviewing your case to determine the best possible assistance."

Now respond to this customer:

"My flight was delayed 5 hours and I missed my connection. I want a refund now!"


# chain-of-thought prompt
------------------------------------------
You are a travel support assistant.

Analyze the customer issue step-by-step before responding.

Steps:
1. Identify the customer’s main issue
2. Determine whether refund conditions may apply
3. Maintain a professional and empathetic tone
4. Provide a final response

Customer message:
"My flight was delayed 5 hours and I missed my connection. I want a refund now!"
